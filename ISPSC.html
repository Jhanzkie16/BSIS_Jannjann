About ISPSC

ISPSC is a comprehensive six-campus institution of higher learning mandated to give professional and technical training both in the undergraduate and graduate levels in fields such as economics, agriculture, engineering, education, management and more. Santa Maria, Candon City, Narvacan, Santiago, Tagudin, Cervantes.
