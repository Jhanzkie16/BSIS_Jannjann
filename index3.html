Cayapa Falls

Hidden within the lush forests of Quirino, Cayapa Falls is a breathtaking cascade that flows gracefully into crystal-clear pools. Surrounded by towering cliffs and vibrant greenery, it offers a serene escape for nature lovers and an invigorating adventure for those who seek to explore its refreshing waters.
